Hydration for Your Daily Routine

COSRX Advanced Snail 96 Mucin Power Essence is a Korean skincare essence formulated with snail secretion filtrate. It is intended to help hydrate the skin and support a smoother-feeling complexion.

Its lightweight texture makes it suitable for layering with other skincare products. Individual results can vary depending on skin type and routine.

Key Ingredient

Snail Secretion Filtrate: A commonly used ingredient in Korean skincare products intended to support skin hydration.

Refer to the product packaging or manufacturer's official ingredient list for the complete and current formulation, especially if you have ingredient sensitivities.

How to Use

  1. Cleanse your face and apply toner if used in your routine.
  2. Apply a small amount of essence to your face.
  3. Gently pat until absorbed.
  4. Follow with moisturiser and sunscreen during the day.

Follow the product label and patch-test new products if your skin is sensitive.
