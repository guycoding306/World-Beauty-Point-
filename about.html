Our Story

Inspired by Korean Beauty

Korean skincare has introduced many people to a more thoughtful approach to everyday beauty, with routines that focus on hydration, cleansing, sun protection and caring for different skin needs.

World Beauty Point brings together Korean skincare products in one convenient shopping destination for customers in Nepal. From familiar skincare favourites to products worth exploring, our aim is to make browsing and choosing beauty essentials easier.

We want customers to feel comfortable exploring products at their own pace, understanding what they are buying and building routines that suit their preferences.
