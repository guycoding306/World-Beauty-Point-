Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about shopping with World Beauty Point.

How can I place an order?

Browse our shop, choose your preferred products, add them to your cart and proceed to checkout to enter your delivery information.

How do I choose the right skincare product?

Start by considering your skin type, skincare goals and any ingredients you prefer to use or avoid. Check product descriptions and usage instructions before purchasing.

How can I ask about my order?

Select "Order Enquiry" in the contact form and include your order number, if available. This will help our team identify your enquiry.

Where can I find delivery and return information?

Delivery charges, delivery availability and return eligibility should be confirmed against the store's published policies. You can also contact the store for clarification.
